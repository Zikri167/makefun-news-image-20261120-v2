Entry for the make.fun competition [Sum up 20 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261120).

## What the image shows

A dark, typography-only poster for Friday 20 November 2026. The lead story is the first-ever trilateral summit in Shenzhen, where Trump, Xi Jinping and Putin issued a joint peace declaration at APEC, drawn with three abstract flag bars. Five numbered stories follow: the ICJ ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya (9–6), Cyclone Akvan hitting the Odisha coast near Gopalpur with more than 300,000 people evacuated, a Houthi missile striking Riyadh's tallest tower while a tanker hit a mine in the Strait of Hormuz, European leaders signalling readiness to negotiate with Russia over Ukraine as Kyiv was hit by ballistic missiles, and NASA's Voyager 1 reaching one light-day from Earth after 49 years.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the bundled `entry.png` poster to the output directory named by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, defaulting to `/output`. The run is offline and deterministic: every run writes exactly the same bytes.
